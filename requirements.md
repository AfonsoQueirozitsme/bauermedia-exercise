The Task

#

Prepare a 20-minute presentation covering:

A self-contained, automated deployment with
terraform of a web hosting environment.

Create a K3s or K8s Kubernetes cluster.

Host a functional website within the cluster.

Implement Load Balancing to ensure service
availability.

The solution may run locally or in the cloud.

All code, commit history, and documentation
must be hosted in a public GitHub
repository.

Be prepared for 15–20 minutes of follow-up
questions from the panel.

Ground Rules & Expectations

#

Please limit your presentation to 20 minutes

Maximum 10 slides. You may use PowerPoint or
similar presentation format.

You are expected to perform a brief Live Demo of the
automated deployment and the functional website.

The use of LLMs is permitted to assist in the process.

We are not looking for perfection. We are looking for
structured thinking.

Expected preparation time: 2–3 hours

We are not expecting: Deep technical diagrams,
Heavy design artefacts.

We are looking for: A slide with an arquitecture
diagram, a slide with high-level components, clear
explanation of the rational of the technical decisions.
